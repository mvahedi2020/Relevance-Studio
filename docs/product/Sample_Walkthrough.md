# Reproduce the original learner story

1. Start with an empty browser sample. The landing page labels a fictional scenario, deterministic rules and no trained model/history. No recommendations appear yet.
2. Choose **Choose my starting point**. Keep **Make sense of data**, **60 minutes**, **Foundational**, with no completed courses. Choose **Preview preferences**. Inspect the before/after limits and Undo scope. Cancel first to observe no save, then preview again and **Confirm change**.
3. Expect this complete order: Read a small dataset → Tell the story behind a number → Ask a better learning question → Notice what the data leaves out. Open **Why this: Read a small dataset**: match 3/3, score 3 × 10 = 30, duration 30 ≤ 60, foundational, no prerequisites and all eligibility checks passing. Escape returns focus to its opener.
4. Select **Exploration**, inspect and confirm the policy preview. Expect ethics → story → questions → foundations. Why shows breadth × 10 + match; no model has been trained.
5. Choose **Not for me: Read a small dataset**. It immediately hides only that course, with local-save status. **Restore Read a small dataset** returns it if other limits qualify. **Undo last change**, preview and confirm, reverses just that last restoration; the snapshot is consumed. There is no history stack.
6. Edit preferences to **20 minutes**, preview and confirm. No eligible courses appear. The idea-sketch course has zero data match and is not a fallback. **Inspect catalog eligibility** shows all eight courses and explicit exclusions. **Edit my preferences** to 60 minutes, preview and confirm, then **For your goal** recovers qualifying results; restore the hidden foundations course as needed.
7. **Policy comparison** is frozen, independent of current choices. Four eligible courses, three useful. Goal top-three useful 3/3 and coverage 3/3; exploration 2/3 and 2/3; both rejected 0/3. Inspect all eight judgments and the ineligible rejected outline.
8. **Reset sample** shows a clearing preview. Cancel preserves choices. Confirm reset returns to cold start. A reviewed **Undo last change** restores the prior valid choices, policy and hidden courses; confirmation consumes Undo. Reload removes Undo.
9. **Refresh saved choices** previews replacement of current tab work, then **Confirm load** applies the reviewed snapshot and clears Undo without offering restoration. A second tab changing saved content after preview causes confirmation to reject as stale, even at the same revision.
10. With malformed or unsupported saved data, inspect the warning: raw data is retained. Tab-only choices can continue. Only confirmed reset replaces corruption, including corruption introduced in another tab without a reload. An unavailable storage getter/read or failed write keeps current choices in this tab and warns of refresh loss.

This walkthrough demonstrates software behavior, not a human study or learning outcome. See [Validation](Validation.md) for executed checks and release gates.
