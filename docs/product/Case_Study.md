# A starting point the learner can question

A new learner lacks history and sees no reason to trust an unexplained course list. Mo's product direction asks what can be useful and safe with little evidence. This original Northstar prototype answers with declared preferences, eligibility before ranking, inspectable reasons and reversible control. AI assisted implementation and testing; it is not an employer implementation, proprietary ranking system or customer study.

The main decision is to require a positive stated-goal match as well as duration, level and prerequisites. With a data goal and only 20 minutes, a 20-minute idea-sketch course has match 0/3. Filling that screen would imply relevance without evidence. The prototype instead explains no match and offers constraint editing and the whole catalog. Browsing remains a viable alternative for a learner who prefers to inspect everything.

Goal fit favors the strongest declared match; exploration favors authored breadth within the same boundaries. The explanation drawer shows the exact calculation and reasons for qualification or exclusion. Immediate not-for-me feedback excludes only the named course, can be restored and does not imply model retraining. Preference and reset changes are reviewed before saving. Raw-content checks reject a stale preview even if another tab retains the same revision.

The independently calculated fictional fixture has four eligible courses, three judged useful. Goal fit's top three covers 3/3 useful eligible courses and includes 3/3 useful shown; exploration covers 2/3 and includes 2/3. Both contain 0/3 rejected shown. Exploration introduces the neutral ethics course. The rejected outline is excluded by zero goal match. These figures describe one authored persona, eight authored judgments and a chosen top-three cutoff; they cannot establish real relevance, engagement or commercial performance.

Product tradeoffs remain provisional: the positive-match threshold may overexclude useful adjacent learning; breadth scores require learner evaluation; declared completions can be inaccurate; one-step Undo is simpler but offers limited recovery. [Decisions and risks](Decisions_and_Risks.md) records alternatives. [Validation](Validation.md) separates software evidence, publication gates and proposed human research. No research, personal review or commercial outcome is claimed.
